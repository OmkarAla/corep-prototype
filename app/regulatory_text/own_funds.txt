CRR Article 36(1)(c):
Deferred tax assets that rely on future profitability shall be deducted from CET1 capital.

PRA Own Funds 2.2:
Firms must apply deductions for DTAs dependent on future taxable profit.

COREP C01.00 Instructions:
Row 030 corresponds to deductions from CET1 including deferred tax assets.
Row 010 corresponds to Common Equity Tier 1 capital.
Row 020 corresponds to Additional Tier 1 capital.
Row 040 corresponds to Tier 2 capital.
